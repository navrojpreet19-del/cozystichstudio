How long does it take to ship my crochet order?

Granny square bags and bouquets are typically processed within 3-5 business days. Custom-fit cardigans are completely tailored to your measurements and take 2-3 weeks to meticulously hand-stitch before shipping.

Can I request custom yarn colors for bags and cardigans?

Yes, absolutely! We love custom requests. You can write your color choices directly in the custom details form section above, or drop us a DM on Instagram to pick your exact yarn colors before checkout.

What comes inside the Premium Gift Bundle Package?

Our premium packages are curated for gift-giving! They include your chosen item neatly wrapped, a beautifully formatted handwritten note with your custom message, and a miniature craft magazine detailing care instructions for your items.
